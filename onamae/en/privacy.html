Privacy Policy

Name Sticker Print · Effective: 2026-11-03

Names stay on this device only. The app does not import photos, show ads, run analytics, or send data to our servers. Purchases and restores use Apple’s services. If you share a PDF, the recipient handles it under their own terms.

Names and layouts

Names, furigana, romaji, child profiles, motif choices, and sheet layouts are processed and stored on device only. Nothing is uploaded to our servers. There is no account.

Purchases

Product lookup, purchase, restore, and entitlement checks use Apple StoreKit. Apple processes transactions. We do not receive or store your Apple payment details.

Sharing and printing

Exported PDFs stay on device until you use the system share sheet or print UI. Recipients follow their own privacy policies.

Data use

We do not track users and do not include advertising or analytics SDKs. The app does not access the photo library.

Children

The app helps label belongings for school or daycare, but it is not designed to collect children’s personal data. Entries remain on device.

Contact

We update this page and the effective date when the policy changes. Contact moonwfa@icloud.com. Do not email files that contain personal data.
