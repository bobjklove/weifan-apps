隐私政策

拼豆图纸·原大打印 · 生效日期：2026-10-09

本应用不设自有账号，不收集或上传个人信息、照片或作品，不使用分析、广告或崩溃上报服务，也不集成第三方 SDK。

照片与作品

你可以通过系统照片选择器、文件选择器或相机导入图片。图片在设备本机处理，不会上传给开发者。图纸、作品数据、降采样且去除元数据的源图及缩略图保存在本机 App 容器的 Application Support/Projects/ 中。你可在应用内删除作品；删除 App 时，随 App 容器保存的作品文件也会被系统删除。应用不会修改或删除相册中的原图。

导出文件在本机生成，不包含源照片，也不包含照片元数据。你主动使用系统打印或分享功能后，文件会交给你选择的 App、服务或打印流程；其后续处理受相应提供方的条款和隐私政策约束。

你也可以主动导出作品文件（.pindou），用于备份或换手机。作品文件包含图纸数据、降采样且去除元数据的源图和缩略图，若使用自定义色卡还包含该色卡；它只在你通过系统分享或“文件”App 保存时离开本机。你创建的自定义色卡同样只保存在本机。

本机保存的信息

免费完整导出的计数和用于避免同一内容重复计次的导出指纹保存在设备本机 Keychain。指纹用于识别导出内容，不是照片文件副本，不会发送给开发者。Keychain 记录与作品文件分开管理；删除作品不会重置免费导出计数，卸载 App 后 Keychain 记录也可能继续保留。应用不向开发者收集照片、作品内容、文件名或付款资料。

购买

商品查询、购买、恢复购买和购买权益核验通过 Apple StoreKit 进行，由 Apple 处理。系统购买流程可能连接 Apple。开发者不会接收或保存你的 Apple 付款信息；Apple 对交易及账户信息的处理适用 Apple 的隐私说明。

网络与第三方服务

本应用自身不向开发者服务器发送网络请求，也不使用分析、广告或崩溃上报服务。系统购买流程可能连接 Apple；你主动分享文件、打印或打开网页/邮件联系支持时，所选系统 App、服务或打印流程可能按其规则处理信息或连接网络。

儿童隐私

本应用不以收集儿童个人信息为目的。图片和作品由使用者主动选择，并仅在本机处理。

政策变更与联系

政策变更时会更新本页面和生效日期。隐私问题请联系：moonwfa@icloud.com。

Privacy Policy

Bead Pattern: True Size Print · Effective date: October 9, 2026

This app has no developer account system. It does not collect or upload personal information, photos, or projects, and it does not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or third-party SDKs.

Photos and projects

You can import an image through the system photo picker, file picker, or camera. Images are processed on your device and are not uploaded to the developer. Patterns, project data, a downsampled source image with metadata removed, and thumbnails are stored locally in the app container at Application Support/Projects/. You can delete projects in the app. When you delete the app, the system removes project files stored in its app container. The app does not modify or delete the original image in your photo library.

Exports are created on your device. They do not contain the source photo or photo metadata. If you choose to print or share an export using a system feature, the file is handed to the app, service, or print workflow you select. Its subsequent handling is governed by that provider's terms and privacy policy.

You can also export a project file (.pindou) for backup or to move to another device. A project file contains the pattern data, a downsampled source image with metadata removed, a thumbnail, and any custom palette the project uses. It leaves your device only when you save or share it through the system share sheet or the Files app. Custom palettes you create are also stored only on your device.

Information stored on your device

The free complete-export count and an export fingerprint used to avoid counting the same content twice are stored in the device's Keychain. The fingerprint identifies export content; it is not a copy of the photo and is not sent to the developer. Keychain records are managed separately from project files. Deleting a project does not reset the free-export count, and a Keychain record may remain after the app is uninstalled. The app does not collect photos, project contents, filenames, or payment details for the developer.

Purchases

Product lookup, purchases, restore purchases, and entitlement checks use Apple StoreKit and are handled by Apple. The system purchase flow may connect to Apple. The developer does not receive or store your Apple payment information. Apple's privacy information applies to its handling of transaction and account information.

Network and third-party services

The app itself sends no network requests to developer servers and uses no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. The system purchase flow may connect to Apple. If you choose to share a file, print, open a webpage, or email support, the system app, service, or print workflow you select may process information or connect to the internet under its own rules.

Children's privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. Images and projects are selected by the user and processed on the device.

Changes and contact

This policy and its effective date will be updated when the policy changes. For privacy questions, contact moonwfa@icloud.com.
