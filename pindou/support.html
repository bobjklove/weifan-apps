支持与常见问题

拼豆图纸·原大打印 · 使用、打印与购买帮助

如何开始？

从系统照片选择器、文件或相机导入图片，在本机生成图纸；按需调整图案后，可查看色号和用料清单，并进入拼搭模式。

如何按 1:1 原大尺寸打印？

导出原大底稿后，在打印对话框中选择“实际大小”或“100%”，关闭“适合页面”及类似缩放选项。应用提供格距校准设置；请按自己的板子完成校准，并在正式拼搭前打印校准页、用尺子核对。不同 PDF 应用、打印驱动和纸张设置可能改变输出尺寸。

照片会上传吗？

不会上传给开发者。照片在本机处理，作品保存在设备本机。系统购买流程由 Apple 处理；你主动使用系统分享、打印或打开链接时，所选 App 或服务按其规则处理。

免费导出如何计算？

完整导出免费 3 次。同一导出内容不会重复计次；计数信息保存在本机 Keychain，与作品文件分开。删除作品不会重置计数。

如何恢复购买？

在应用的解锁页面选择“恢复购买”，并使用购买时的 Apple 账户。购买和恢复通过 Apple StoreKit 处理。如果仍有问题，请联系支持邮箱。

购买价格是多少？

一次买断，没有订阅。价格以你所在地区 App Store 购买界面显示为准。

如何联系支持？

邮件：moonwfa@icloud.com。描述问题时可提供设备型号、iOS 版本和复现步骤；请不要通过邮件发送包含个人信息的照片或作品文件。

Support & FAQ

Bead Pattern: True Size Print · Help with use, printing, and purchases

How do I get started?

Import an image from the system photo picker, Files, or the camera. Create a pattern on your device, adjust it as needed, review color codes and the materials list, and follow the colors in assembly mode.

How do I print at 1:1 actual size?

After exporting the actual-size template, choose “Actual Size” or “100%” in the print dialog and turn off “Fit to Page” and similar scaling options. The app provides board-pitch calibration. Calibrate for your board, print the calibration page, and check it with a ruler before assembling. PDF apps, printer drivers, and paper settings can change the output size.

Are my photos uploaded?

Photos are not uploaded to the developer. They are processed on your device, and projects are stored locally. Apple handles the system purchase flow. If you choose to share, print, or open a link, the app or service you select handles it under its own rules.

How do free exports work?

The first three complete exports are free. The same export content is not counted twice. The count is stored in the device Keychain, separately from project files, so deleting a project does not reset it.

How do I restore a purchase?

Choose “Restore Purchases” on the unlock screen and use the Apple account used for the purchase. Apple StoreKit handles purchase and restore. Contact support if you still need help.

What is the price?

It is a one-time purchase with no subscription. The price for your storefront is shown in the App Store purchase sheet.

How do I contact support?

Email moonwfa@icloud.com. You can include your device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce an issue. Please do not email photos or project files containing personal information.
