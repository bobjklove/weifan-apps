Privacy Policy

Photo Print Size · Effective date: 2026-10-04

Photo Print Size does not collect personal data and does not include third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Photos and layouts

The app accesses only photos you select with Apple's photo picker or explicitly authorize. Photos are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. We do not receive or store photo files, image content, photo-library originals, or filenames on our servers. The app does not modify or delete photo-library originals.

The app stores the current layout draft and the photo copies it needs on your device so you can resume your work. You can clear drafts and those copies in Settings; this does not delete photo-library originals.

When you use Fill, Apple Vision may detect face locations on your device to suggest a crop. With multiple faces, a suggestion prioritizes the largest face. Face locations are not saved or uploaded. A draft stores only the final crop center and whether it came from an automatic suggestion, a manual adjustment, or a center fallback. It does not store face boxes, facial features, or other face data. A manual crop stays in place unless you request a new suggestion.

Purchases

Product lookup, purchases, restore requests, and entitlement checks use Apple StoreKit. Apple processes the transactions. We do not receive or store your Apple payment information. See Apple's privacy materials for its handling of transaction and account information.

Sharing and printing

Exported PDFs are saved on your device. If you share a PDF through the system share interface, it is sent to the app, service, or print workflow you select. The recipient's own terms and privacy policy govern its handling.

Data use and services

We do not track users or operate photo-upload, account, advertising, or analytics services for the app. To count distinct free exports, a layout identifier is derived from the full bytes of each selected photo file and the layout geometry. The identifier is a hash, not an image file: it contains no photo-file bytes or filenames, stays on your device, and is not sent to us. Apple system frameworks provide photo selection and PDF functions; Apple processes StoreKit transactions.

Children

The app is for general audiences and is not designed to collect children's personal information. A user selects photos through Apple's system picker, and processing takes place on the device.

Website links and hosting

The app contains links to this privacy policy and the support site. If you tap one, your browser visits that website; the browser and website host may process technical request information under their own terms. We do not add analytics or tracking scripts to these static pages. Photos are not uploaded by the app when you open a website link.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date if the policy changes. Contact moonwfa@icloud.com with privacy questions. Please do not email photos or private PDFs.
